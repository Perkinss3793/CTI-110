# Sandwich Construction

## Assumptions
- We have an unobstructed countertop
- On the counter we have:
    - a plate with two slices of bread side-by-side
    - jar of PB
    - jar of jelly
    - a butter knife
    - napkin
    - the robot is charged and ready to go
<br><br><br>

## Steps to build the sandwich
1. Open the PB
2. Open the jelly
3. Pick up the butter knife
4. Use the knife to scoop the PB from the jar
5. Spread PB on left slice of bread
6. Use napkin to clean the butter knife
7. Use the knife to scoop the jelly from the jar
8. Spread jelly on right slice of bread
9. Use napkin to clean the butter knife
10. Discard napkin
11. Lay down the butter knife
12. Stack the bread with PB & Jelly sides touching
13. Replace the lids on PB & Jelly
14. Put PB, Jelly, and knife away

<br><br><br>

## Exit Conditions
- The plate and constructed sandwich are the only items on the counter
